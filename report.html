WHAT IS FOCUS 1.2?

The FinOps Open Cost and Usage Specification (FOCUS™) is an open-source technical standard supported by the FinOps Foundation. It defines a unified billing schema that allows engineers to normalize raw, proprietary cost exports from AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, and others into a single, consistent ledger without losing context.
